Dayadhvam Damyata

From T.S. Eliot's The Waste Land — the three commands of the thunder

Datta

दत्त — "Give"

The thunder speaks first of generosity. What have we given? Not the prudent calculation of benefit, but the moment of surrender that an age of prudence can never retract.

Dayadhvam

दयध्वम् — "Sympathize"

Each in their own prison of self, we hear the key turn once. Compassion is the bridge — to confirm a prison is to acknowledge the other who stands outside it.

Damyata

दम्यत — "Control"

The boat responds gaily to the hand expert with sail and oar. Self-mastery is not suppression but the discipline that sets the spirit free.
